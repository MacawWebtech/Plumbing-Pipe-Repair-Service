Stay safe

What not to do

Don’t use hot water

If the heater may be damaged, hot water can make things worse.

Don’t touch wet electrics

Switch off at the board if it is safe – never stand in water.

Don’t force valves

Stuck valves can snap and turn a small leak into a flood.
